This Master Vehicle Rental Agreement and Terms of Service ("Agreement") is a legally binding contract between you (the "Hirer", "Customer", or "You") and DriveLuxe Technologies Private Limited ("DriveLuxe", "Company", "We", or "Us"), governing your access to and use of our website, mobile interfaces, vehicle reservation systems, and physical vehicle fleets.

Driver Eligibility, KYC & Document Verification

To ensure road safety and statutory compliance under the Motor Vehicles Act, every driver operating a DriveLuxe vehicle must satisfy the following minimum criteria:

  • Age Requirement: Minimum 21 years of age for Economy, Sedan, and Compact SUV categories. Minimum 25 years of age for Premium, Luxury Executive, and Sports categories (e.g. Porsche 911, Audi A4, BMW 5 Series).
  • Valid Driving License: You must possess an original, valid, non-probationary Government Driving License (LMV - Light Motor Vehicle) held for at least 12 consecutive months. Learner licenses are strictly ineligible.
  • Identity & Phone Verification: Account registration requires an authenticated mobile number via one-time password (OTP), proof of identification (Aadhaar, Passport, or National ID), and valid address verification.
  • Sole Authorized Driver: Only the registered customer who uploaded their driving license is legally authorized to drive the vehicle, unless additional drivers were explicitly added during the booking wizard and approved by DriveLuxe.
Strict Liability Warning: Allowing an unauthorized, unverified, or third-party individual to drive the vehicle immediately voids all insurance coverage, terminates the rental contract, incurs a ₹25,000 breach penalty, and makes the registered hirer 100% financially and criminally liable for all damages or third-party claims.

Reservations, Dynamic Pricing & Invoicing

DriveLuxe operates an automated, dynamic rental system. Your rental quote is calculated based on:

  1. Base Daily Rental Rate: Standard 24-hour rate for the selected vehicle model.
  2. Multi-Day Tier Discounts: Automated reductions applied for extended rental periods.
  3. Selected Add-ons: Daily charges for optional additions including GPS navigation systems, infant/child safety seats, Wi-Fi connectivity, and registered secondary drivers.
  4. Insurance Protection Tier: Daily premium corresponding to your chosen coverage package (Basic, Standard, or Premium).
  5. Coupons & Promotional Credits: Legitimate coupon codes applied at checkout prior to payment processing.

All quotes are inclusive of statutory GST. Toll taxes (FASTag), inter-state entry taxes, parking fees, and fuel costs are the sole responsibility of the customer.

Refundable Security Deposit & Inspection Protocol

A mandatory refundable security deposit is held prior to vehicle dispatch or handover to protect against incidental expenses, traffic penalties, late returns, or minor damages:

  • Standard / Economy Fleet: ₹5,000 to ₹10,000 security deposit.
  • Executive / Luxury / Sports Fleet: ₹15,000 to ₹35,000 security deposit.

Return Inspection & Refund Timeline:

Upon vehicle return at a designated DriveLuxe hub (Downtown Hub, Airport Express Terminal, Luxury Lounge, etc.), a joint digital vehicle inspection is conducted recording odometer readings, fuel/battery level, exterior scratch scan, and interior upholstery condition.

Automated Refund Guarantee: Once the return inspection is completed with zero damage and verified fuel levels, DriveLuxe initiates the 100% refund of your security deposit within 24 to 48 banking hours directly to your original payment method (Bank Account / Card / UPI).

Fuel Policy, EV Charging & FASTag Toll Charges

  • Fair Fuel Policy (Like-to-Like): Vehicles are provided with a verified fuel level (typically 100% full tank) and must be returned at the exact same level. If returned with less fuel, the differential is charged at prevailing market rates plus a ₹250 refueling convenience charge.
  • Electric Vehicles (EV) Battery Policy: EVs are handed over with at least 85% battery charge. Hirers must return the EV with at least 20% battery charge to avoid battery deep-discharge recovery penalties.
  • Electronic Tolls (FASTag): All DriveLuxe vehicles are equipped with active commercial FASTags. All toll plazas, airport parking fees, and expressway charges incurred during your rental are logged and deducted cleanly from the security deposit, with detailed digital receipts provided.
  • Traffic Violations & Fines: Any e-challans, speeding tickets, signal jumping fines, or towing charges generated by traffic authorities during your rental timeframe will be forwarded to your registered email and charged to your account.
8

GPS Telematics, Speed Limiting & Safety Monitoring

For passenger safety, fleet security, and statutory insurance compliance, all DriveLuxe vehicles are fitted with secure onboard telematics devices recording:

  • Real-time GPS location and route tracking.
  • Vehicle speed and excessive overspeeding alerts (>120 km/h or statutory state highway limits).
  • Collision and impact sensor data for automated emergency SOS dispatch.
  • Engine health, battery status, and diagnostic error codes.
Speed Advisory: In accordance with Central Motor Vehicles Rules, speed is monitored. Continued severe overspeeding exceeding 130 km/h triggers an automated warning, and repeat violations incur a ₹2,500 speed governance fine to protect driver and passenger lives.

Prohibited Vehicle Uses & Breach Penalties

The hirer agrees NOT to use or permit the vehicle to be used for:

  1. Commercial passenger ferrying (taxi, ride-share) or commercial goods haulage.
  2. Sub-leasing, renting, or transferring the vehicle to any third party.
  3. Speed testing, drag racing, drifting, rally competitions, or driving instruction schools.
  4. Transportation of illegal substances, weapons, inflammable chemicals, or contraband under the NDPS Act.
  5. Towing or pushing any other vehicle, trailer, or motorized equipment.
  6. Driving outside the Republic of India or into restricted military/border territories without prior written approval.

Accidents, Mechanical Breakdown & Roadside Assistance

In the rare event of a mechanical malfunction, flat tire, or road collision:

  1. Safety First: Ensure all passengers are safe and turn on hazard flashers.
  2. Call DriveLuxe Emergency Concierge: Immediately contact our 24/7 Roadside Hotline at +91 90000 10001 or tap "Emergency Assistance" in the Customer Portal.
  3. Accident Reporting: In case of accident or third-party damage, filing a First Information Report (FIR) at the nearest Police Station is mandatory for all insurance claim processing. Never admit liability or accept settlement offers without DriveLuxe representative presence.
  4. Replacement Vehicle: If breakdown occurs due to mechanical failure unrelated to customer misuse, DriveLuxe dispatches a replacement vehicle or arranges complimentary alternate transit.

Our Privacy Pledge & Regulatory Compliance

DriveLuxe Technologies Private Limited ("DriveLuxe", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information and upholding your fundamental right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data collection, processing, encryption, and sharing practices in compliance with:

  • The Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India).
  • The Information Technology Act, 2000 and IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.
  • International data protection benchmarks including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
2

Categories of Information We Collect

To deliver seamless car rental experiences, we collect the following data categories:

  • Identity & Profile Information: Full legal name, username, email address, mobile phone number, profile photograph, and date of birth.
  • Government Driving Credentials: Driver's license number, scanned driving license images (front/back), issuing state transport authority, and validity dates.
  • Transactional & Financial Data: Order IDs, booking invoices, rental durations, payment gateway transaction tokens (Razorpay / Stripe), coupon code history, and security deposit refund records. (Note: We never store your raw credit card numbers or CVVs on our servers).
  • Vehicle Telemetry & GPS Tracking: Live vehicle geolocation, travel speed, distance traveled, and trip start/end timestamps during active rental windows.
  • Technical & Browsing Data: IP address, device operating system, browser type, search logs, and vehicle model preference clickstreams to improve our AI recommendation system.

Driving License & Identity Verification Security

Under Indian Motor Vehicle Regulations, DriveLuxe is legally required to verify that every driver operating a rented vehicle holds an authentic, active driving license.

All uploaded driver's license scans and avatar images are processed through automated validation checks and stored in encrypted, private cloud storage buckets. Access to license documents is restricted strictly to authorized DriveLuxe compliance officers under multi-factor authentication for statutory verification and insurance claim settlement purposes.

Vehicle Telematics & Live GPS Geolocation Policy

DriveLuxe vehicles are equipped with telematics IoT hardware. We collect location data solely for:

  1. Assisting emergency roadside rescue teams in locating your vehicle during breakdowns or collisions.
  2. Enabling real-time theft prevention, unauthorized cross-border detection, and stolen vehicle recovery.
  3. Auditing accurate odometer readings for fair rental billing and electronic FASTag toll reconciliations.
Privacy Safeguard: Telematics logs are linked only to active rental booking IDs. Once a trip is completed and reconciled, granular real-time GPS telemetry is archived into cold encrypted storage with access strictly restricted.

Lawful Bases & Purposes of Data Processing

We process your personal information on the following legal grounds:

  • Contractual Necessity: To process vehicle bookings, facilitate pickup/drop-off, generate invoices, and execute security deposit refunds.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: To maintain statutory motor vehicle hire registers and furnish documentation required by the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways (MoRTH).
  • Legitimate Interests: To detect fraud, prevent fleet theft, secure our network infrastructure, and enhance fleet recommendations.
  • Customer Consent: To send booking SMS alerts, OTP verification codes, and optional promotional loyalty rewards (which you can opt-out of at any time).
6

Data Storage, Cloud Infrastructure & Encryption Standards

DriveLuxe implements bank-grade security protocols to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is protected using 256-bit Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Encrypted Database Storage: Enterprise PostgreSQL database with encrypted data partitions and robust Row Level Security (RLS) configurations.
  • Private Supabase Storage: Sensitive assets (profile photos, driver verification records) are stored in secure private buckets and delivered only via short-lived, cryptographically signed URLs.
  • Tokenized Authentication: Secure JSON Web Token (JWT) architecture ensuring credentials are never exposed in transit.
7

Third-Party Disclosures & Data Transfer

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for advertising purposes. We share data only with trusted partners strictly necessary to operate our service:

  • Payment Processors: PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateways (Razorpay and Stripe) for secure authorization and refund transactions.
  • Insurance Providers: Regulated insurance underwriters in the event of accident assessment or claims processing.
  • Emergency Roadside Partners: Certified breakdown recovery teams dispatched to assist you during transit.
  • Law Enforcement Authorities: Only when compelled by valid legal subpoenas, court orders, or statutory police investigation notices.
8

Your Data Rights & "Right to be Forgotten"

Under data protection laws, you possess comprehensive rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: You can view all your stored personal details, booking history, and invoices anytime via the Customer Portal.
  • Right to Rectification: You can edit your contact information and profile details directly in your account settings.
  • Right to Erasure (Account Deletion): You may request the deletion of your account and personal profile by emailing privacy@driveluxe.in.

Note on Statutory Retention: In compliance with statutory tax audits and transport regulations, financial invoices and completed rental trip logs are retained for 7 years as mandated by law before permanent purging.

Cookies & Local Storage Usage

We do not deploy third-party advertising tracking cookies. You can clear your browser cookies and local storage at any time by clicking "Log Out" in the user menu.
